Example screen: it's Mira's turn in a half-explored dungeon, and her torch is flickering. Black-and-white graph paper throughout, with coloured player tokens as the only colour. Light shows only in the torch tracker, and dark rooms are hatched. The same screen is shown in light mode and in dark mode ("chalk on slate").

What to look at

  • Light per room: the chapel is lit by Mira's torch, so it shows plain graph paper. The entry hall and corridor have no light and are hatched over the grid. Brann is standing in the dark there.
  • Doorway stop: Mira's token stops at the east door. The room behind it is unknown (dashed "?") until the GM reveals it.
  • Tokens: players are coloured discs with initials, and the goblin (G1) is an ink disc. Mira holds the spotlight, so her token has a thick ring and a pulse, and a small flame shows she carries the light.
  • Flame levels are shown only in the torch tracker: a text label and three bars.
  • Monsters: always black discs. In dark mode they get a stronger light outline so they stand out against the slate paper.
